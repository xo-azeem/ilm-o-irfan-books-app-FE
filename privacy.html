Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

Ilm o Irfan is a reading app published by Ilm o Irfan Publishers ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who else sees it, and what you can do about it. It covers the Ilm o Irfan Android and iOS apps.

In short. We collect what is needed to keep an account, remember your place in a book, and deliver a membership you have paid for. We do not show advertisements, we do not use an advertising identifier, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.

What we collect

Your account

When you create an account we store your email address and, if you choose to fill them in, your name, phone number, date of birth, postal address and a profile picture. Everything except the email address is optional and can be cleared at any time from Profile → Edit profile.

If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's email address, name and profile picture. We never receive your Google password.

Signing in

Each active sign-in is recorded so that Profile → Signed-in devices can show you where your account is in use and let you sign a device out. That record holds the device model and operating system version, the app version, and when the session was last active.

Your reading

So that a book opens where you left it and your library follows you between devices, we store your reading progress (the book, the page, how far through you are), your highlights and notes, your saved books, which books you have downloaded for offline reading, your reading streak and daily reading activity, the achievements you have earned, and your monthly reading goal.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a device notification token from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging along with the device name, platform and app version, so we can send the alerts you have switched on. Turning notifications off in the app removes the token.

Membership and payments

Memberships are sold and billed by Google Play or the Apple App Store, never by us directly. We receive a record of whether your membership is active, which plan it is, when it renews or expires, and the store's transaction identifiers. We never see your card number or any other payment details.

Support

If you write to us, we keep your message and email address for as long as it takes to answer you and to keep a record of the answer.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifier. The app does not request Android's advertising ID and contains no advertising SDK.
  • No location data of any kind.
  • No contacts, calendar, microphone or camera access.
  • No access to your photos. The app asks Android for two legacy storage permissions on older versions; they are used by a library that detects screenshots, not to read your gallery.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking across other apps or websites.

Why we use it

Purpose What it uses

Remembering your reading Progress, highlights, saved books, downloads, streaks

Who else sees it

We use a small number of service providers. They process data on our instructions only, and none of them is permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Supabase Database, sign-in and file storage — where the data lives
Google Google sign-in, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications
RevenueCat Keeps track of membership status from the app stores
Resend Sends account emails — confirmations, codes, membership notices
Google Play / Apple Take the payment and manage the subscription

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising. We will disclose data only if the law requires it.

What you can do

  • See it. Profile → Privacy & security → Download my data gives you a copy of everything your account holds.
  • Correct it. Profile → Edit profile.
  • Delete it. Profile → Privacy & security → Delete account. A request is reviewed, then there is a seven-day grace period during which you can still change your mind. After that the account and the personal data in it are removed. An active membership must be cancelled with the store first, so that you are not left paying for an account that no longer exists.
  • Sign other devices out. Profile → Privacy & security → Signed-in devices.
  • Stop notifications. Profile → Notifications, or in your phone's settings.

How long we keep it

Account and reading data is kept while your account exists. When the account is deleted, it is removed. Records we are obliged to keep — a purchase record, for instance — are kept only for as long as the law requires, and are held separately from your account.

How it is protected

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Each reader's rows are isolated at the database level, so one account cannot read another's. Books downloaded for offline reading are stored encrypted on your device, and screenshots and screen recording are blocked inside the app to protect the publishers' work.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page, and for a significant change we will tell you in the app.
